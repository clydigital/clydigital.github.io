POWER STACK INVESTMENT WORKFLOW

From a market story to an investment decision.

A compelling headline is a research lead. A credible investment view needs the company's own records, a valuation and a portfolio-level decision.

SOURCE HIERARCHY

What qualifies as evidence?

01 · PRIMARY

Company disclosure

Original 10-Q/10-K or local-regulator statements, SEC XBRL, issuer presentations, results and full earnings-call source references.

02 · RECONCILIATION

Check the economics

Align fiscal periods and accounting definitions. Compare cash from operations, capex, debt, financing obligations and management guidance.

03 · INVESTMENT

Valuation meets risk

Source-dated price and FX, bear/base/bull assumptions, catalysts, thesis invalidation and verified holdings before capital allocation.

THEME · AI ECONOMICS

AI investment is not automatically AI profit.

Separate rising capex from incremental free cash flow, segment demand and customer concentration. Cloud revenue growth may coexist with declining near-term cash conversion; financing terms and returns matter.

Verification path: earnings disclosures → cash-flow statement → management's capital commitments → subsequent quarters.

THEME · RATES / FX

Policy expectations can reprice earnings.

Rates influence discount rates and borrowing costs; the dollar can also change MYR-denominated investment returns. Avoid treating spot market moves as confirmed future cash flow.

Verification path: Treasury and central-bank releases → financing disclosure → current listing/currency quote → portfolio sensitivity.

THEME · ENERGY

Supply shocks pass through unevenly.

Oil prices, freight and refined-product costs affect producers, consumers and transport businesses differently. The transmission mechanism is more useful than treating all energy exposure as a single trade.

Verification path: official inventory and supply data → company-level cost sensitivity → pricing power and balance sheet.

THEME · TECH COMPETITION

China-US AI competition creates winners and risks.

Semiconductors, memory, power infrastructure and policy restrictions can change supply and margins. Distinguish domestic substitution from profitable execution; source access differs across exchanges.

Verification path: issuer and local-exchange filings → capacity/market-share evidence → pricing, cash generation and valuation.
